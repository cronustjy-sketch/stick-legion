# STORY.md — Stick Legion

## Presentation
- Story is told with **story cards**: a short in-engine scene using the real 3D stickmen and props, posed
  (no complex animation), with a slow camera pan and 1–3 lines of text at the bottom. Click/tap to advance, Skip button.
- Cards play: before Chapter 1 (prologue), before each chapter's first level, after each boss, and the ending.
- Bosses get a short taunt banner when they appear, and one line when defeated.
- Mark cards as seen in the save file. A "Story" button on the main menu replays any seen card.
- English only. Keep every line short (max ~20 words).

## Setting
The kingdom of **Aurum**: green valleys, stone fortresses, gold banners.
The enemy is the **Crimson Host**, an army in dark iron and red cloth that came out of the eastern mountains.

## Characters
- **Aldric** (the player hero): captain of Aurum's last legion. Gold scarf, steel armour, longsword.
- **Corvin**: Aldric's older brother. Once Aurum's greatest general. Now the **Crimson King**.
  Taller than Aldric, dark iron armour, red cape, a black greatsword.
- **King Edmar**: the old king of Aurum. Only appears in the prologue and the Chapter 3 reveal.
- **Mira**: the legion's quartermaster. Gives the player tips and short lines between chapters.

## Prologue (before Level 1)
1. *Aurum's capital burns. Gold banners fall.* — "Aurum has fallen. The Crimson Host came from the east, and no wall could stop them."
2. *Aldric stands alone in the ashes, holding a torn gold banner.* — "One captain survived. One banner still stands."
3. *Mira and a handful of soldiers gather around him.* — Mira: "Captain, we're all that's left. Tell us where to march."
4. *Aldric raises the banner.* — Aldric: "We take it back. One valley at a time."

## Chapter 1 — The Green Valleys (Levels 1–5)
Intro card: *A small border village under red banners.* — "Every village we free joins the legion."
- Lvl 1–4: tutorial-paced battles, new units unlock (archer lvl 2, spearman lvl 3, shield knight lvl 4).
- **Boss lvl 5 — Grask the Butcher**: a huge brute with two cleavers. Charges in straight lines, spins.
  - Appears: "Little gold soldier. I've butchered better."
  - Defeated: "The King... will finish... what I started."
- After boss card: *Grask's red banner burns.* — Mira: "He said 'the King'. The Host has a king?"

## Chapter 2 — The Iron Pass (Levels 6–10)
Intro card: *A mountain pass blocked by a red siege fortress.* — "To reach the heart of Aurum, we must break the Iron Pass."
- New units: mage (lvl 6), assassin (lvl 7), priest (lvl 8).
- **Boss lvl 10 — Siege Master Volk**: rides a giant siege engine, fires boulders (telegraphed red circles), calls catapults.
  - Appears: "Walls, armies, kings. I've crushed all three."
  - Defeated: "You fight like him. Like the King. Same eyes."
- After boss card: *Aldric finds a gold-and-red medallion on Volk's engine. It's an Aurum general's medal.* — Aldric: "This belonged to an Aurum general... Only one general ever wore this."

## Chapter 3 — The Burning Fields (Levels 11–15)
Intro card: *Golden wheat fields on fire.* — "The Host burns everything it cannot hold."
- New units: giant (lvl 11), cavalry (lvl 12), catapult (lvl 13).
- **Boss lvl 15 — The Twin Blades, Sera and Kade**: two fast duelists fighting together. When one falls, the other enrages.
  - Appear: "The Crimson King sends his regards, little brother of his."
  - Defeated: "Ask your old king... what he did to Corvin."
- **Reveal cards** (after boss):
  1. *Flashback, warmer colours: young Aldric and Corvin training side by side.* — "Corvin. My brother. Aurum's greatest general."
  2. *King Edmar on his throne, Corvin kneeling.* — "When the eastern war was won, the King feared him. He called him a traitor and sent him to die in the mountains."
  3. *Corvin alone in the snow, red cloth wrapped around a wound.* — "He didn't die. He came back with an army."
  4. *Present day. Aldric holds the medallion.* — Aldric: "The Crimson King... is Corvin."

## Chapter 4 — The Crimson Throne (Levels 16–20)
Intro card: *The ruined capital, now under red banners. A dark throne in the old palace.* — "The road ends where it began. At home."
- Hardest battles: mixed enemy armies, bigger waves, enemy gets every unit type.
- Mira (before lvl 18): "Whatever he was to you, Captain, the legion follows you. Not him."
- **Boss lvl 20 — Corvin, the Crimson King**: 3 phases.
  - Phase 1: greatsword combos, ground slam.
  - Phase 2 (66%): summons a ring of red elite guards.
  - Phase 3 (33%): fights alone, faster, desperate. Red sky.
  - Appears: "Little brother. You picked up the banner I dropped."
  - Phase 3: "Aurum threw me away! Why do you still bleed for it?"
- **Final choice card** (after he's defeated): *Corvin on one knee, sword broken. Aldric stands over him.*
  Two buttons: **Spare him** / **End it**.

## Endings
**Spare him**
1. *Aldric lowers his sword and offers his hand.* — Aldric: "Aurum wronged you. Help me build a better one."
2. *Gold and red banners side by side over the rebuilt capital.* — "The two armies became one legion. The brothers rebuilt Aurum together."
3. — "THE END. Ending: Brothers"

**End it**
1. *Aldric strikes. The red banner falls.* — Corvin: "...You were always the loyal one."
2. *Aldric alone on the throne steps at sunset, gold banner raised.* — "Aurum was free. But its captain never smiled again."
3. — "THE END. Ending: The Crown"

Both endings unlock **Endless Mode** and show credits: "A game by Junyuan".
Save which endings were seen; show "Endings 1/2" or "2/2" on the main menu.

## Mira's tips (random line on the level-select screen)
- "Spearmen eat giants for breakfast."
- "Shield knights block arrows. Put them in front."
- "Hold Tab to see the whole battle."
- "Priests behind the front line keep everyone alive longer."
- "Catapults are slow, but fortresses hate them."
- "Use Follow and lead the charge yourself."
